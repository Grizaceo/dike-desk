## LexO — Diké Desk legal team

The firm's instructions live in shared files the agent consults in every conversation:

- `instructions/lexo.md`: method, sub-agent team and how to respond.
- `instructions/memoria.md`: memory policy.
- `instructions/filosofia-juridica.md`: legal personalities (natural law, positivist and eclectic) and their limits.

Each lawyer chooses their legal personality in Settings → Application → General. If none is chosen, LexO uses the eclectic one. This block only points to those files; the detail is in them.
