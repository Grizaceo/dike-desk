# NIF and the logic of norms

The **Normative Interaction Frame (NIF)** is the team's common format. In Diké Desk it is a documentary artifact: there is no NIF board and it does not update itself. Whoever delegates hands it over explicitly; whoever receives it works on it, not on free text, and returns it updated. Do not store it in shared resources or memory: it belongs to the brief and its conversation.

## NIF fields

```text
facts:             known / alleged / missing, with a date when it matters
parties:           role (plaintiff, defendant, public body, court, third party)
norms:             reference with locator, rank and status in Diké (verified / ambiguous / refused / stale / not cited)
acts:              contracts, resolutions, judgments, official letters
principles:        those guiding interpretation
deontic positions: obligation / permission / prohibition / power, with its holder
conflicts:         between norms, acts or criteria; open / resolved, and by which rule
exceptions:        those that can change the outcome
gaps:              what the legal order or the evidence does not cover
hypotheses:        H1–H4, with their decisive factor
evidence for / against: source, locator, passage, verification status
```

Fill in only what applies, but say what is missing. The share of filled fields is only a minimum indicator: a missing indispensable fact, source or milestone prevents a material conclusion even if the rest is complete.

## How to read a norm

Break each provision into:

1. **Operative facts:** when it applies.
2. **Legal consequence:** what it produces.
3. **Addressee:** whom it obliges, permits or empowers.
4. **Deontic operator:** obligation, permission, prohibition or power.
5. **Condition of application:** time limits, requirements and competence.
6. **Exception:** what displaces it.

## Relations between norms

| Relation | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Hierarchy | Which has higher rank? |
| Competence | Which body may regulate the matter? |
| Speciality | Which regulates the case more specifically, once hierarchy, competence, temporal validity and scope are checked? |
| Temporality | Which governs on the relevant date? |
| Cross-reference | Does one complete the other? |
| Conflict | Are they incompatible on these facts? |
| Repeal or prevalence | Did one set the other aside, in whole or in part? |
| Gap | Does neither cover the case? |
| Defeasibility | Which exception or principle defeats it on these facts? |

A conflict is resolved by stating the rule used (hierarchy, competence, speciality, temporality or interpretation). Distinguish repeal, displacement and uncertainty: they are not the same. If it cannot be resolved, it stays open and is escalated to the lawyer.
