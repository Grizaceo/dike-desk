# Procedural deadlines (guide)

Diké Desk has no calendar and no alerts. This skill guides; it does not replace the firm's deadline control.

## Data needed before computing

1. The event that starts the period and its exact date (usually the valid notification, not the date of the decision; the norm may set another event).
2. The type of proceeding and the court or authority.
3. The step: appeal, answer, observations or another.
4. The norm that sets the period.

If any of the four is missing, ask for it and do not compute.

## Counting

1. Identify the norm and its text; if it does not have `verified` status in the current record, mark it `[VERIFICAR CITA]`.
2. Determine whether the norm sets working days or calendar days, and how it treats holidays and suspensions. Do not presume it: it depends on the body of law and the proceeding.
3. Compute step by step, showing the first day, each exclusion and the last day.
4. Account for holidays or suspensions only with a source that proves them.
5. If you have no verifiable means of computing for the case (a proven holiday calendar, a rule for hours or months), give only the counting method and ask for confirmation: a natural-language calculation is not deterministic.

## Deliverable

- Milestone, norm and status.
- Data used and data missing.
- Step-by-step calculation, or the method if it cannot be computed safely.
- Risks: doubtful notification, unconfirmed holiday, recently amended norm.
- A final warning: the date is a supporting calculation the lawyer must confirm.
